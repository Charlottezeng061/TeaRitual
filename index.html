About Tea Ritual

Tea Ritual is a small interactive experience inspired by Chinese tea-making.

Drag the hand-drawn tools and follow the guide to prepare and share a cup of tea.

Through gentle movement and simple actions, this project invites you to slow down and enjoy a little moment for tea.
